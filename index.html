Velora help

Quick answers

How do I claim?

Complete checkout, keep your order number, then use the claiming page or Discord ticket instructions.

How long does delivery take?

Usually around 30 minutes when staff and bots are available. Please allow up to 1 day during busy periods.

Why is payment pending?

VeloraMM2 manually checks each payment before delivery. Your page updates when staff verifies it.

What if I cannot join VIP?

Add the delivery bot shown on your delivery page, keep trades enabled, then tell staff you sent the bot a friend request.

VIP troubleshooting

If the VIP link will not open, add the bot first, check Roblox privacy settings, rejoin Roblox, and keep the claiming page open so staff can see your order status.

Who do I contact?

Email support@veloramm2.com or open a Discord ticket with your order number.
